Dear students,

Regarding the Sessional-I exam, please remember that the syllabus remains focused up to Exercise 3.3. To perform well, concentrate on the following topics:

1. Function Domains and Ranges: Understand where functions work and the values they can take. Recognize even and odd functions and their symmetries.

2. Function Transformations: Learn how to manipulate functions through translation, scaling, and composition, and find equations for translated functions.

3. Limits and Continuity: Grasp the concepts of limits and how functions behave when they are continuous.

4. Graph Asymptotes: Identify and work with various types of asymptotes in graphs, including horizontal, vertical, and oblique asymptotes.

5. Differentiable Functions: Develop proficiency in graphing differentiable functions, finding derivatives using basic rules, and determining equations for tangent and normal lines.

6. Piecewise Functions: Understand the concept of differentiability in piecewise functions.

By focusing on these specific aspects of the topics, you'll be well-prepared for the exam.
