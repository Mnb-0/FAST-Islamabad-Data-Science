### Instructions Regarding Functional English Lab Exam
- State: PUBLISHED

Instructions Regarding Functional English Lab Exam

1. The exam spans two hours. The paper is divided into three sections. Attempt all the sections. Ensure thorough comprehension of the questions before providing your responses. 
2. Section A comprises two listening tests. 
Test One is subdivided into four parts. For attempting test one, clear instructions are provided in the audio. 
3. Test Two, on the other hand, consists of a single part, and you will be granted 3 minutes to read the questions before the audio is played because the audio has no prior instructions for attempting the test. 
4. Section B comprises a reading comprehension test and Section C is based on Grammar. 
5. Make sure to be in the exam hall at least 10 minutes prior to the scheduled exam time as the audios will be played only once. Late arrivals can lead to distractions and may impact the clarity of the audio, affecting the students’ ability to attempt the test effectively. 
6. Carefully read and follow the directions given before each question.
7. Take careful notes with a pencil during the audio presentation for rough work, which can be later used to address the associated questions.
8. Do not forget to transfer your answers to the designated answer table given at the end of each section; otherwise, the answers will not be considered for evaluation. 
9. Any section completed with a soft pencil, overwritten or rewritten will NOT undergo marking and cannot be subject to reevaluation.


Regards,
