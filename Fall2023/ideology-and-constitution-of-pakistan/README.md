### Final Project
- Due: 2023-11-17 18:59 UTC
- State: PUBLISHED

Purpose of the final Project:
The purpose of the project is to create awareness among society regarding social and current issues. In social sciences, Society is considered the lab for observations and experiments for the students of social sciences. Therefore, the students are required to visit random places and societies to observe the social issues and opinions of the people. Intellectually, it is a healthy activity for the students of social sciences to learn the art of communication and fieldwork through this project. Besides this, the students can create awareness among people regarding social issues. 

Procedure: 
All the students are divided into groups. Each group has to make a video (Documentary) of 10 to 20 minutes. The content of the video would be based on the assigned topics. The group members are instructed to ask topic-related questions from random people to fulfill the requirements of the project. First of all, the group members have to introduce themselves in the video and then continue the video by asking questions from random people. Once you are done with the task, conclude your video with the experience that you get from the project and also mention whether the activity was helpful or not. All the students have to participate in the project and video.

Instructions for the Project:
1. First of all, make at least 15-20 questions regarding the assigned topic and show them to your instructor.
2. Select an open area (Marketplace, Parks, shopping malls, universities, and institutions) for your project and avoid going to rural areas.
3. Before starting the interview, introduce yourself with your institutional background and the purpose of the project so that people feel comfortable answering your questions.
4. Do not make fun of people if they are not able to answer your questions.
5. Some people would get emotional or aggressive during interviews, in such cases, no need to be panic just leave them and move on.
6. Do not use unethical language during your interviews.
7. Be professional while asking your questions.
8. Keep in mind that the purpose of the project is to create awareness about social and current issues in Pakistan amongst people.
9. As it is a trend on social media (TikTok, Facebook, YouTube) so most people will not be ready to answer your questions. Assure them that the purpose of recording video is an academic activity and it won’t be published or shared anywhere.
10. Avoid asking personal questions.

How to Start the Project?
Step 1: Make a group of 5-7 students
Step 2: Select a group leader who will be in contact with the instructor regarding the project activity.
Step 3: The group leader will assign activities to the students.
Step 4: Decide a day, time, and place for the project activity and inform your colleagues.
Step 5: Edit the video and make it a short documentary. Add your names to the video with credits. Give a title to your documentary and upload it on GCR. 
Note: All the group members are strictly instructed to coordinate with the group leader.
