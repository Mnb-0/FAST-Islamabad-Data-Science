Final Year Project (FYP) – Important Announcement

Dear
Final Year Project Students,

The Final Year Project (FYP) process has been revamped by the School of Computing to align with the evolving landscape of computing. The revised process incorporates the latest tools, emerging technologies, and contemporary coding and development trends to ensure that your FYP experience reflects current industry and academic practices. To help you clearly understand the revised process and the expectations associated with your FYP, a Reference Document has been prepared and attached to this announcement. All students are required to carefully review this document before finalizing and registering their
projects.

Emphasis on Complex Computing Problems

One of the key additions to the revamped FYP process is the explicit incorporation of the concept of a Complex Computing Problem. The reference document provides detailed guidelines regarding what constitutes a complex computing problem and
how your project should address the relevant characteristics. Therefore, students are required to define and structure their proposed FYP in accordance with the guidelines provided in the reference document. Please make sure that your project idea, problem statement, scope, and proposed solution are aligned with these requirements before proceeding with registration.
