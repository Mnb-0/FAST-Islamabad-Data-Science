- Due: 2026-06-23 18:59 UTC

SUBMIT CVs FOR THE FOLLOWING OPPORTUNITY - BE SURE TO CUSTOMIZE CV ACCORDING TO NADRA REQUIREMENTS


The NADRA Student Internship Program 2026 is designed to provide university students with immersive, hands-on exposure to a complex, high-security government enterprise environment. Interns will work alongside experienced NADRA professionals, contribute to live operational and project work, and develop competencies directly aligned with their academic disciplines.
2. Eligible Academic Discipline
NADRA invites nominations from students currently enrolled in the following disciplines. Preference shall be given to candidates demonstrating strong academic standing, leadership potential, and a commitment to public service.
a. Computer Science
b. Software Engineering
c. Cyber Security
3. Nomination Requirements
To facilitate an efficient and merit-based selection process, NADRA requests the following:
Ensure that each resume includes the student's complete contact information, CGPA, current semester, and discipline.
Each institution may nominate multiple candidates; however, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for further assessment.
The deadline for submission of nominations is 23rd.
It is important to mention that NADRA reserves the right to select interns solely on merit. Submission of a nomination does not guarantee selection. Only shortlisted candidates will be notified.
4. Selection Process and Terms
The selection of interns shall be conducted through a transparent, merit-based process. NADRA's Human Resource Directorate, in coordination with relevant departments, will review submitted applications and may conduct telephonic or in-person interviews before final placement.
The following terms shall govern the internship engagement:
Selected interns will be assigned to NADRA offices based on discipline and operational requirements.
Each intern will be assigned a designated supervisor who will guide, mentor, and evaluate their performance throughout the programme.
Interns will be required to observe NADRA's Code of Conduct, confidentiality obligations, and data security protocols at all times.
A performance-based completion certificate will be issued to all interns who successfully fulfill the programme requirements.
The lump-sum stipend of Rs. 25,000/- shall be disbursed upon successful completion and submission of an end-of-internship report.
